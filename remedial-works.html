What we fix

Common remedial works

These are the actions that come up most often in Legionella risk assessments. If it's in your report, we can quote for it.

Cold water tank replacement

Old, corroded or oversized tanks swapped for WRAS-approved tanks with tight-fitting lids, insect screens and screened overflows and vents.

Tank cleaning & disinfection

Drain-down, removal of sediment and biofilm, chlorination and flushing, with a disinfection certificate to BS 8558.

Tank lids, insulation & screens

Upgrade existing tanks so dirt, insects and heat can't get in, keeping stored cold water below 20°C.

Dead leg removal

Cut out redundant pipework where water sits still and bacteria thrive. One of the most common, and most effective, fixes.

Little-used outlet removal

Remove or re-plumb outlets nobody uses, so you're not flushing them every week forever.

Pipe insulation

Lag hot pipes to keep them hot and separate or insulate cold pipes that are warming up near heat sources.

Hot water temperature fixes

Reset and verify cylinder and calorifier temperatures to at least 60°C, and balance circulation so outlets reach 50°C within a minute.

Calorifier & cylinder cleaning

Drain-down and blow-down to remove sludge from the base of hot water vessels, where Legionella can multiply.

System disinfection

Whole-system chlorination after a positive sample, new installation or major works, certified to BS 8558.

TMV installation

Fit thermostatic mixing valves where scalding is a risk, so hot water can be stored safely hot. More on TMVs.

Shower head & hose replacement

Replace scaled or perished shower heads and hoses with easy-clean, low-retention fittings.

Expansion vessel upgrades

Replace dead-end expansion vessels with flow-through types, or fit drain valves so they can be flushed.

Prioritising actions

What needs doing first?

How we prioritise remedial actions. Your assessor will set the priority for each action in your report.

PriorityWhat it meansTypical timeframe
HighA clear risk of exposure, e.g. contaminated tank, hot water below 50°C, no control schemeImmediately, within days
MediumConditions that could support growth, e.g. dead legs, warm cold water, uninsulated pipeworkWithin 1–3 months
LowImprovements and good practice, e.g. labelling, replacing ageing fittingsPlanned maintenance

A guide only. Timeframes depend on who uses the building. Healthcare and care settings may need faster action.

FAQs

Remedial works questions

Do I have to complete every action in my report?

You must take reasonable steps to control the risk. High-priority actions should be dealt with straight away. Lower-priority items can often be planned in, and we'll help you build a sensible programme.

Will the water need to be turned off?

For some works, yes, but usually only for a short time. We'll tell you in advance and work around your tenants, residents or business hours.

Can you work in occupied homes and HMOs?

Yes. Much of our work is in occupied rented property. We give tenants notice, keep disruption to a minimum and leave everything clean.

Will you update my risk assessment afterwards?

Yes. When we've completed the work, we record it against your assessment so you can show the action has been closed off.
