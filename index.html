Your legal duty

Who needs a Legionella risk assessment?

If you're responsible for a building with a water system, whether as employer, landlord, managing agent or facilities manager, the law expects you to identify and control the risk from Legionella.

That duty comes from the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 and the COSHH Regulations 2002, with the practical detail set out in the HSE's Approved Code of Practice L8 and technical guidance HSG274.

  1. 1

    Identify and assess the risk

    A competent person must assess the water system and record the significant findings.

  2. 2

    Prepare a written control scheme

    Spell out what is checked, how often, and by whom.

  3. 3

    Put controls in place

    Temperature control, flushing, cleaning and remedial works to remove the risk.

  4. 4

    Keep records and review

    Log your checks, and review the assessment regularly or whenever anything changes.

FAQs

Questions we get asked a lot

Is a Legionella risk assessment a legal requirement?

Yes. Employers, landlords and anyone in control of premises must assess and control the risk of exposure to Legionella. The law doesn't require a certificate, but you do need to show a suitable assessment has been carried out and acted on, and a written assessment is the simplest way to prove it.

Is Legionella testing the same as a risk assessment?

No. A risk assessment looks at the whole water system and how it's managed, to find where bacteria could grow. Water sampling is a lab test of the water at a moment in time. Sampling is useful to check your controls are working, but it doesn't replace an assessment.

How often should my risk assessment be reviewed?

The HSE says it should be reviewed regularly and whenever there's reason to think it's no longer valid: after changes to the system, a change of use or occupancy, or a positive sample result. Many duty holders review every two years as good practice. We'll recommend a review date in your report.

How long does the site visit take?

A typical flat or house takes under an hour. Offices, schools and care homes vary with size and complexity, and we'll give you a realistic estimate when we quote.

What do I get at the end?

A written report with your risk rating, prioritised actions, a schematic of the water system and a written control scheme. If remedial work is needed, we'll price it separately with no obligation.
