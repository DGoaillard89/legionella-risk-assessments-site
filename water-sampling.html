Legionella water sampling & testing

Sampling gives you laboratory proof that your control measures are working. Our engineers take samples the right way, from the right outlets, and we explain the results in plain English, along with exactly what to do if anything comes back high.

What we test for

Laboratory analysis we arrange

Legionella

Culture testing for Legionella species, including Legionella pneumophila, the main cause of Legionnaires' disease.

Total viable counts (TVC)

A general measure of bacterial activity in the water, used to spot problems in tanks and systems early.

E. coli & coliforms

Indicators of contamination, used for drinking water checks and after tank cleaning or disinfection.

Pseudomonas aeruginosa

Required in augmented care areas in hospitals under HTM 04-01, where patients are most vulnerable.

Dental unit waterlines

Microbial testing of dental waterlines to support HTM 01-05 compliance and CQC inspections.

Post-disinfection checks

Samples after chlorination or tank cleaning to confirm the system is clean before it goes back into use.

When is sampling needed?

Sampling isn't always required, but sometimes it's essential

  1. Your assessment says so

    Where temperature control is hard to achieve, or where users are particularly vulnerable.

  2. Healthcare & care

    Routine sampling in hospitals, augmented care and some care settings, as part of the Water Safety Plan.

  3. After works or a positive result

    To confirm disinfection has worked, or to track a system back to safe levels.

  4. Investigating a concern

    Complaints about water quality, a suspected case, or a system that's been out of use.

Understanding results

What your Legionella results mean

A simplified summary of the HSE's suggested actions for hot and cold water systems (HSG274 Part 2). We'll interpret your results for your specific site.

Result (cfu per litre)What it suggestsWhat happens next
Not detected / under 100Controls are workingCarry on with your monitoring programme
100 to 1,000Control may be slipping, especially if several samples are positiveResample, review your control measures and risk assessment, and fix any problems found
Over 1,000Significant colonisationResample urgently, review controls straight away, and consider disinfection of the system

A summary only. Always follow the advice for your site, and act sooner if users are vulnerable.

FAQs

Water sampling questions

Does a negative result mean my water is safe?

Not on its own. A sample is a snapshot of one outlet at one moment, and Legionella can be present elsewhere in the system. That's why sampling supports a risk assessment and monitoring programme rather than replacing them.

How long do results take?

Legionella is cultured in the lab, so results usually take around 10 days. Tests such as TVC and coliforms are quicker. We'll let you know as soon as results are in.

Do landlords need to test for Legionella?

Not usually. For most rented homes, the HSE doesn't expect routine sampling. A good risk assessment and simple controls are enough. We'll only recommend sampling if there's a real reason for it.

How many samples will I need?

It depends on the size of the system and why you're sampling. We'll recommend the number and location of samples when we quote.
