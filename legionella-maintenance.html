Legionella maintenance & monitoring

What gets checked, and how often

Typical routine checks for hot and cold water systems, based on HSE guidance HSG274 Part 2. Your written scheme sets the exact tasks for your building.

TaskWhat we checkTypical frequency
Sentinel outlet temperaturesHot reaches at least 50°C (55°C in healthcare) within 1 minute; cold below 20°C within 2 minutesMonthly
Hot water cylinder / calorifierFlow at least 60°C, return at least 50°CMonthly
Little-used outletsFlushed to draw fresh water throughWeekly
Shower heads & hosesDismantled, cleaned, descaled and disinfectedQuarterly
Cold water storage tanksIncoming and stored water temperatures6-monthly
Cold water storage tanksVisual inspection for debris, lids, screens and conditionAnnually
CalorifiersInspection and drain or blow-down where neededAnnually
Representative outletsRotating sample of other outlets, so every tap is checked over timeAnnually
TMVsTemperature, fail-safe and service. See TMV servicing6–12 monthly
Risk assessmentReview, and after any significant changeRegularly

Frequencies are typical, not fixed. Your risk assessment may set different ones.

FAQs

Maintenance questions

Can my own staff do some of the checks?

Yes, as long as they're competent and properly trained, and the checks are recorded. Many clients have site staff do weekly flushing while we handle the monthly and annual tasks.

What happens if a reading fails?

We tell you the same day, explain the risk and recommend the fix, whether that's a thermostat adjustment, extra flushing or a remedial job. If sampling is needed, we can arrange it.

Do I still need a risk assessment?

Yes. The assessment and written scheme decide what needs monitoring. If you don't have a current one, we can do both.

Is there a minimum contract?

Talk to us. We'll recommend a plan that fits your building and budget.
